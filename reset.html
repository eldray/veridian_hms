Reset database

This erases the hospital database and rebuilds it as a brand new installation.

Everything in this database will be permanently deleted:
  • patients, attendances, bills, claims and every clinical record
  • all staff accounts. Only a new administrator is created.
  • stock levels, tariffs and settings you changed (reference data is reloaded as shipped)

Other PCs lose access to Veridian HMS until the reset finishes.
